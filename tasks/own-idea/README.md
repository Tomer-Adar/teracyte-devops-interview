# Your own idea

**Bonus task — your choice of category** · [Assignment](../../README.md) · Time guide: your call (aim for ~1–2 h)

## Goal

Show us something we didn't ask for: one DevOps improvement that makes this system cheaper, faster, safer, more reliable, or easier for Dev or Data/ML to work with.

## Deliverables

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| The change | Your repository |
| The write-up | EVIDENCE.md, section "Bonus: own idea" |

## Requirements

1. The improvement must address a real problem in **this** system: the code, the setup, or the workflow in this repository. Not a generic best practice.
2. It must not overlap with another task or bonus in this assignment.
3. Use an established tool where one fits. If you write code, explain why no tool fit.
4. The write-up must have exactly four parts:
   1. **Problem:** what is wrong or slow today, who it affects, and how you observed it.
   2. **Change:** what you did, and which tool you used.
   3. **Evidence:** a before/after measurement, or a failure that no longer happens, with the commands used.
   4. **Limitation:** one thing it does not solve, or one risk it adds.
5. It counts as one of your three bonuses.

## Done when

- [ ] All four parts of requirement 4 are present, and the evidence includes commands a reviewer can re-run.

## Evidence to include in EVIDENCE.md

- The four-part write-up.

## Not required

- Anything large. A small change with clear evidence scores higher than a big change without it.
