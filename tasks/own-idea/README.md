# Your own idea

**Bonus task — your choice** · [Assignment](../../README.md)

## Goal

Show us something we didn't think to ask for: a DevOps improvement that makes this system cheaper, faster, safer, more reliable or easier for Dev or Data/ML to work with. Any relevant work counts.

## Requirements

If you choose this task:
- State the problem in this system that your change solves, and who benefits.
- Implement it, preferring an established tool where one fits.
- Show before/after evidence: a measurement, a failure that no longer happens, a step that got removed.
- It counts as one of your three bonuses.

Small and well-evidenced beats large and unproven.

## How to demonstrate it

A short section in EVIDENCE.md with the problem, the change, the evidence and one limitation.
